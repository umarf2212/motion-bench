What this design includes

The actual circuit mapping comes from this project's micropython/main.py and README.md: SDA = GPIO4, SCL = GPIO5; left/right/move/scroll = GPIO0/1/6/7, each switched to ground using the firmware's internal pull-up.

The PCB file contains plated through-hole pads, assigned nets, routed copper and an exact 76.20 × 101.60 mm outline. The HTML uses the same coordinates and nets. It is a carrier for your existing modules, not a bare-chip ESP32 design.

Battery power circuit

1 · Cell & protection

Cell + / − → J6.1 / J6.2.
J5.1 → charger B+.
J5.2 → charger B−.
J5.3 → charger OUT+.
J5.4 → charger OUT−.

2 · Switch & regulation

OUT+ → J7 SPST switch → J8.1 regulator IN+.
OUT− → GND → J8.2 regulator ground.
Regulator 5 V OUT+ → J8.3.

3 · Isolate & supply

J8.3 → JP1.1.
Removable shunt → JP1.2 → C3 5V.
C3 3V3 → sensor VCC.
C1 = 100 nF; C2 = 100 µF.

The TP4056 is a charger, not a 5 V regulator. A full cell reaches about 4.2 V: never connect it directly to the 3V3 pin. This design intentionally adds a regulated 5 V boost stage instead of assuming your C3 regulator works across the full cell discharge range. B− and OUT− must remain separate so the protection circuit is not bypassed.

Assembly sequence

  1. Cut isolated-pad perfboard to at least 76.2 × 101.6 mm. Mark column 1 and row 1. Check the 1:1 print ruler before using it as a template.
  2. Dry-fit both modules. U1 uses two 1×8 sockets, 2.54 mm pitch, 15.24 mm between rows. From the USB end: left 5/6/7/8/9/10/20/21; right 5V/GND/3V3/4/3/2/1/0. Stop if your labels differ.
  3. Install sockets and landing headers. Do not force a 4-leg tactile switch into a 2-pin header footprint. Connect one terminal from each electrically separate pair, and confirm the switch closes only when pressed.
  4. Wire the ground and signal connections using the checklist. Use insulated wire for crossings. No wire, solder bridge or battery under the C3 antenna keepout.
  5. With both modules removed and no power, check for shorts between supply rails and ground; capacitors may briefly appear conductive. Check every listed endpoint and each switch.
  6. Test from ESP32 USB first with JP1 removed and the battery switch OFF. Confirm 3.3 V at the sensor supply, then verify sensor and buttons in Motion Bench.
  7. Separately verify cell polarity, charger protection, charge-current setting and boost output under load. Confirm a regulated 5.0 V at JP1.1 before fitting the shunt. Disconnect ESP32 USB before battery operation.
  8. Secure the charger, regulator and cell with insulating spacers/support. The module boxes are placement reservations, not drill templates. Keep solder joints away from the pouch cell.

Known limits

The photo cannot establish exact switch footprints, charger module hole positions, battery discharge rating, regulator model or clone-specific power isolation. Those parts connect via wire pads here.
